What the data says
TrendSales have levelled off
Sales for 1–25 October are 257,803, up 1.5% on the same days of September (254,101) and 25% below August (343,603).
MarginOctober margin looks better than it is
No bandwidth payment is recorded this month. At about 65,000 a month (130,000 every two months), operating margin falls from 84% to about 58%.
CapitalEquipment explains the year-to-date loss
Hardware is 2,260,564 of 2,952,964 spent (77%). Excluding it, year-to-date operating margin is 32.4%, not the -188% on the dashboard.
PaybackRoughly 14 months to recover equipment
August to October operating profit averages about 157,000 a month. That is a rough guide and rises if more equipment is bought.
PatternSunday leads, Thursday trails
Sunday averages 11,707 a day and Thursday 8,770, which is 25% lower. Friday to Tuesday sit within 3% of each other.
WatchOne weak day in the last 14
Fri 16 Oct recorded 5,500, about half the 14-day average. Check whether it was an outage or a late entry.
Data quality
Fix firstSales split is estimated
190 of 192 sales rows are Centipid CSV imports with an estimated 50/50 split between mobile money and cash. The income mix and the cash versus Centipid balances are therefore not reliable.
ReviewFour days with no sales
23 to 26 July have no entries. 95 of 99 days are recorded.
ReviewNo money movements recorded
The Movements tab is empty: no Centipid withdrawals and no partner payouts, so balances cannot be reconciled.
ReviewDashboard and entries differ by 500
The Dashboard shows October sales of 257,303 and a 500 "since start" row; the entries total 257,803. Check for a stray 500 entry.
Fixed hereWeekday table in the sheet shows zeros
The "Sales since start" column is 0 for six weekdays and 1,000 for Sunday. This view uses real daily averages instead.
