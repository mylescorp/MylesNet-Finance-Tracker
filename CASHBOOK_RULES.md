# Auditable cashbook event rules

These are the first-release rules for operational records. Amount fields use the workspace currency's integer minor units; legacy field names ending in `Ugx` are retained for compatibility. Every operation is scoped to the authenticated workspace, idempotent, and audit logged.

| Event | Cashbook account effect | Sales/expense effect | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sale | Increase the selected account | Sales income | Existing entry validation and audit trail apply. |
| Expense | Decrease the selected account | Expense | Existing warning and audit trail apply. |
| Cash/mobile transfer | Decrease source; increase destination | None | Source and destination must differ. |
| Mobile-money withdrawal | Decrease mobile money by cash received plus fee; increase cash by cash received | Fee is a separate expense entry | Fee is never included in the cash received amount. |
| External deposit | Increase selected account | None | It is not reported as sales. |
| Partner capital contribution | Increase selected account | None | Partner must belong to the workspace. |
| Savings contribution | Decrease selected business account; increase recorded holding balance | None | A transfer into savings is not an expense. |
| Savings withdrawal | Increase selected business account; decrease recorded holding balance | None | Cannot withdraw more than the recorded holding balance. |
| Loan disbursement | Increase selected business account; increase outstanding principal | None | Loan proceeds are not sales income. |
| Loan repayment | Decrease selected account; reduce outstanding principal | Interest portion only is an expense | Principal cannot exceed the outstanding balance. The monthly payment shown is an estimate using fixed-rate amortization. |
| Bill schedule | No account change | No expense | A schedule is only a reminder; no automatic accrual or payment occurs. |
| Confirmed bill payment | Decrease the selected account | Expense entry using the bill category | Owner confirms the actual date and amount. |
| Reconciliation count | No account change | None | Captures the current book balance and physical count; past dates are rejected because historical balances are not reconstructed. A difference can be explained but not silently adjusted. |

Negative-balance rules use the workspace warning setting and require a separate explicit confirmation. Reconciliation differences do not alter ledger values. Expenses generated by bill payments, loan interest, and movement fees cannot be voided alone; a linked operational reversal must be designed before correcting one of those records. Operational history is filtered by the selected reporting period while current balances and active schedules remain current-state values. A workspace supports up to 100 active savings holdings, loans, bills, and partner records so every active record remains selectable in the current interface.

Partner payouts, automatic bill accruals, interest income, investment revaluation, balance adjustments, and two-person approvals are intentionally not enabled until their approval and correction rules are defined.

## Known limits before source migration

- No workbook rows are imported by these workflows. The workbook schema and row-level reconciliation remain prerequisites to migration.
- The existing external webhook was not changed. Its caller, payload, authentication, retry contract, and staging parity remain unknown and must be documented before any cutover.
- Savings balances represent recorded contributions less withdrawals; they are not valuations and do not include investment gains.
- Loan payments are entered with principal and interest portions supplied by the user. The displayed schedule is a planning estimate, not an authoritative lender schedule.
- Ownership records can be entered and partner contributions can be attributed. Owner-issued invitations bind a partner login to exactly one active partner record; the partner can see only their ownership and attributed capital contribution history. Profit distributions, payout balances, and ownership history are not implemented.
- Correction screens for operational records, off-platform backups, restore, period locking, and provider delivery remain separate production work.
