Add reviewed month-end cashbook closes

Authorized staff can prepare a full-month cashbook and matching-budget snapshot after account reconciliations and data checks pass. A different reviewer must approve it; later changes require an audited reopen reason.

Approved snapshots can be printed or saved as a PDF with recorded account movements, category totals, budget actuals, exclusions, checklist results, and preparer/reviewer history.
