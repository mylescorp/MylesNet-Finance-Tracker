Unify finance period and account filters across workspace pages

The Dashboard and finance pages now share Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Yearly, Last year, and Custom ranges with account filtering in the page action bar. Record forms stay closed until requested from the Record button. Bill schedules support common categories plus a custom category, show an updating due countdown, and advance the next due date after a payment is recorded. Savings supports bulk recording weekly Thursday contributions across a selected date range, with one audited activity row per contribution.
